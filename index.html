        EduGenie Setup Instructions

        Follow these steps to connect EduGenie to your Google Gemini API:

        1. Get your free Gemini API Key from Google AI Studio.
        2. In your project root directory, copy .env.example to .env:
          copy .env.example .env
        3. Open .env and add your key:
          GEMINI_API_KEY=your_actual_api_key_here
        4. Start/restart the server:
          python -m uvicorn app.main:app --reload
